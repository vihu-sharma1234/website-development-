Eating Habits

Grizzly bears are highly versatile omnivores. Despite their reputation as fierce apex predators,vegetation actually makes up the vast majority of their food supply. Lets see their diet:

Plant-Based Food (Approx. 75% of Diet):

Grizzlies consume massive quantities of berries, nuts, roots, leaves, shoots, fungi, and fruit. They use their long, heavy claws and distinctive muscular shoulder humps to easily dig up roots and bulbs.

Animal-Based Protein:

They hunt and forage for insects, rodents, fish (particularly salmon for coastal populations), sheep, elk, moose, and deer calves. They will also readily scavenge on carrion (dead animals) or human garbage if available.

Hyperphagia (Pre-Hibernation Gorging):

During the late summer and fall, grizzlies enter a metabolic state called hyperphagia. Driven to pack on fat reserves for the winter, they can eat up to 90 pounds (40 kg) of food per day, gaining roughly 3 pounds (1.3 kg) of body fat daily. Before settling into their dens.
